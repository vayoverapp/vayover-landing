Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2026

Vayover ("we", "our", "us") operates the Vayover mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Phone number — used for account creation and authentication
  • Profile information — display name, date of birth, gender, country, languages spoken
  • Voice recordings — voice notes you send during conversations
  • Referral codes — if you enter a premium referral code

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information — device model, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage data — session frequency, conversation counts, feature usage
  • Push notification tokens — to deliver notifications

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain the Vayover service
  • Match you with other users based on language and preferences
  • Process and deliver voice notes between matched users
  • Moderate reported content to ensure community safety
  • Send notifications about matches, messages, and account activity
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and enforce our Terms of Service
  • Improve and develop new features

3. Voice Recordings

Voice notes are stored temporarily during active conversations. When a conversation expires (4-8 hours depending on account type), all associated voice messages are permanently deleted.

If a voice note is reported by another user, it is preserved for moderation review. Reported voice notes may be transcribed and analyzed using automated tools to assist human moderators in making decisions.

We do not sell, share, or use your voice recordings for advertising purposes.

4. Content Moderation

To maintain a safe community, reported voice notes may be:

  • Reviewed by human moderators
  • Transcribed using speech-to-text technology (Deepgram)
  • Analyzed by AI systems (Anthropic Claude) for content classification

These tools assist moderators in understanding content across multiple languages. Final moderation decisions are made by human reviewers.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, cloud functions, push notifications
  • Deepgram — transcription of reported voice notes only
  • Anthropic — AI-assisted moderation of reported content only

These services process data solely on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy commitments.

6. Data Retention

  • Voice messages: Deleted when conversation expires (4-8 hours)
  • Reported content: Retained until moderation is complete, then archived
  • Account data: Retained while your account is active
  • Banned accounts: Phone number and device identifier retained to prevent re-registration

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Account deletion permanently removes:

  • Your profile information
  • All conversation history
  • Notification tokens
  • Statistics and achievements
  • Any active matches

You may also contact us at privacy@vayover.app to request data deletion.

8. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (TLS), secure authentication, and access controls. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Vayover is intended for users aged 14 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you believe a child under 14 is using Vayover, please contact us and we will remove their account.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@vayover.app.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by other means. Continued use of Vayover after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
