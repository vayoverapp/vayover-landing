Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Vayover ("we", "our", "the app") is a voice-based anonymous social application. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

  • Phone number — for account creation and verification via SMS OTP
  • Voice recordings — voice notes you send during sessions (stored temporarily for delivery, then deleted)
  • Profile preferences — your selected avatar, display name, conversation preferences
  • Usage data — session participation, timestamps, interactions (reactions, nudges)
  • Device information — device type, OS version, app version (for crash reporting)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To match you with conversation partners based on your preferences
  • To deliver voice messages during active sessions
  • To send push notifications (new matches, messages, session events)
  • To prevent abuse and enforce community guidelines
  • To improve the app experience

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, push notifications
  • RevenueCat — subscription/purchase management
  • Apple/Google — in-app purchase processing

4. Data Retention

  • Voice recordings are deleted after session expiry (within 12 hours)
  • Account data is retained while your account is active
  • You can request full deletion by contacting us

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing us.

6. Children

Vayover is not intended for users under 17 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
