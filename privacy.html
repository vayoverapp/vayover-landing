Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Vayover ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Phone Number

We collect your phone number solely for account authentication via SMS verification. Your phone number is not shared with other users or third parties beyond our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication by Google).

2.2 Voice Notes

Voice notes are transmitted directly between matched users during conversations. We do not store voice notes on our servers under normal circumstances. Voice notes are only temporarily stored when:

  • A user reports a voice note for violating community guidelines
  • The reported voice note is retained only for the duration of the moderation review
  • Once a moderation decision is made, reported voice notes are deleted

2.3 Device Information

We collect a device fingerprint (a non-identifying hash of device characteristics) for the following purposes:

  • Enforcing bans on devices used to violate community guidelines
  • Preventing banned users from creating new accounts on the same device

This fingerprint does not identify you personally and cannot be used to track you across other apps or websites.

2.4 Country

We detect your country from your device locale settings. This is used to:

  • Match you with users from different countries for diverse conversations
  • Display country information during matches (e.g., flag icons)

We do not collect precise location data (GPS coordinates).

2.5 Usage Data

We collect anonymized usage statistics such as:

  • Number of matches completed
  • Total conversation duration
  • Daily activity streaks

This data is used to provide app features (achievements, statistics) and improve the service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the Vayover service
  • Authenticate your account and maintain session security
  • Match you with other users for conversations
  • Enforce community guidelines and moderate reported content
  • Prevent abuse through device-level ban enforcement
  • Provide in-app features such as achievements and streaks
  • Send push notifications about matches and account activity

4. Content Moderation

When a voice note is reported by another user:

  • The reported voice note may be transcribed using automated speech-to-text technology
  • The transcription may be analyzed by an AI system to assess whether it violates community guidelines
  • Human moderators may review the transcription and AI analysis
  • Voice note audio and transcriptions are deleted after the moderation decision is made

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate Vayover:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, cloud functions, push notifications
  • LLM Model: Speech-to-text transcription and AI analysis for content moderation (only for reported content)

These services process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy policies and data protection commitments.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active. Deleted upon account deletion request.
  • Voice notes: Not stored unless reported. Reported voice notes are deleted after moderation review.
  • Device fingerprints: Retained indefinitely for banned devices to prevent circumvention. Non-banned device data is deleted with the account.
  • Usage statistics: Retained as long as your account is active.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Secure cloud infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform)
  • Access controls limiting who can view moderation data
  • Regular security reviews of our systems

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Object to certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

9. Children's Privacy

Vayover is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete that information.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place for any international data transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or by other appropriate means. Your continued use of Vayover after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
